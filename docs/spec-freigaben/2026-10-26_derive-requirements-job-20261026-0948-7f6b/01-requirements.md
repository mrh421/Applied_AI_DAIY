## Requirements

**ANSCHLUSSNEHMER-VERANTWORTUNG** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (1) Satz 1)*
The Anschlussnehmer is responsible towards the Netzbetreiber for the proper erection, extension, modification and maintenance of the electrical installation behind the house connection fuse.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: verify from the contractual and organisational allocation of duties that the Anschlussnehmer is the party answerable to the Netzbetreiber for these activities on the installation behind the house connection fuse.

**VERANTWORTUNG-MESSEINRICHTUNG-AUSNAHME** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (1) Satz 2)*
The Anschlussnehmer's responsibility for the installation does not cover metering devices that are not owned by the Anschlussnehmer.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: verify from the allocation of responsibility that metering devices owned by another party are excluded from the Anschlussnehmer's responsibility.

**VERANTWORTUNG-BEI-UEBERLASSUNG** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (1) Satz 3)*
The Anschlussnehmer remains responsible for the installation when it is let or otherwise left for use, wholly or partly, to a third party.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: verify from the allocation of responsibility for an installation left to a third party that the Anschlussnehmer's responsibility is retained.

**ANLAGE-REGELKONFORMITAET** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (2) Satz 2)*
The installation may be erected, extended, modified and maintained only in accordance with the NAV, other applicable legal provisions, official requirements and the generally recognised rules of technology.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: verify from the design and execution documentation of the installation its conformity with each of the named bodies of rules.

**REGELN-DER-TECHNIK-NACH-ENWG-49** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (2) Satz 3)*
For the generally recognised rules of technology, § 49 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 of the Energiewirtschaftsgesetz applies accordingly.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: verify that the determination of the generally recognised rules of technology follows § 49 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 EnWG.

**ARBEITEN-NUR-DURCH-EINGETRAGENE** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (2) Satz 4)*
Work on the installation may be carried out only by the Netzbetreiber or by an installation company entered in the installer directory of a Netzbetreiber.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: for the work carried out, verify that the executing party is the Netzbetreiber or is listed in an installer directory of a Netzbetreiber.

**EINTRAGUNG-NUR-QUALIFIKATION** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (2) Satz 4 Halbsatz 2)*
The Netzbetreiber may make entry in its installer directory dependent only on proof of sufficient professional qualification for the respective work.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: verify from the entry conditions of the installer directory that the only precondition is proof of professional qualification for the respective work.

**INSTANDHALTUNG-AUSNAHME-EINTRAGUNG** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (2) Satz 5)*
Maintenance work outside the section from the house connection fuse to the metering device is exempt from the installer-directory requirement.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: for maintenance work outside that section, verify that no entry in an installer directory is required.

**INSTANDHALTUNG-MESSSTRECKE-EINTRAGUNG** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (2) Satz 5)*
Maintenance work within the section from the house connection fuse to the metering device, including the metering device, requires the executing party to be the Netzbetreiber or an installation company entered in an installer directory.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: for maintenance work within that section, verify that the executing party is the Netzbetreiber or is entered in an installer directory.

**NETZBETREIBER-ARBEITSUEBERWACHUNG** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (2) Satz 10)*
The Netzbetreiber is entitled to monitor the execution of the work on the installation.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: verify from the arrangements for work on the installation that the Netzbetreiber's right to monitor the execution is provided for and not obstructed.

**PLOMBIERUNG-AUSSTATTUNG-DURCH-ANSCHLUSSNEHMER** *(SRC-de-nav, § 13 (3) Satz 2)*
The Anschlussnehmer must arrange the equipment of the installation required for sealing according to the Netzbetreiber's specifications.
*Check:* no automatic check; at review: compare the sealing equipment of the installation with the Netzbetreiber's specifications and verify that it conforms and was arranged by the Anschlussnehmer.
