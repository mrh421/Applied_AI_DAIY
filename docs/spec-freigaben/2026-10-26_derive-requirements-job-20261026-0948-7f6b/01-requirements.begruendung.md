electrical installation behind the house connection fuse. · VERANTWORTUNG-MESSEINRICHTUNG-AUSNAHME: The Anschlussnehmer's responsibility for the installation does not cover metering devices that are not owned by the Anschlussnehmer. · VERANTWORTUNG-BEI-UEBERLASSUNG: The Anschlussnehmer remains responsible for the installation when it is let or otherwise left for use, wholly or partly, to a third party. · ANLAGE-REGELKONFORMITAET: The installation may be erected, extended, modified and maintained only in accordance with the NAV, other applicable legal provisions, official requirements and the generally recognised rules of technology. · REGELN-DER-TECHNIK-NACH-ENWG-49: For the generally recognised rules of technology, § 49 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 of the Energiewirtschaftsgesetz applies accordingly. · ARBEITEN-NUR-DURCH-EINGETRAGENE: Work on the installation may be carried out only by the Netzbetreiber or by an installation company entered in the installer directory of a Netzbetreiber. · EINTRAGUNG-NUR-QUALIFIKATION: The Netzbetreiber may make entry in its installer directory dependent only on proof of sufficient professional qualification for the respective work. · INSTANDHALTUNG-AUSNAHME-EINTRAGUNG: Maintenance work outside the section from the house connection fuse to the metering device is exempt from the installer-directory requirement. · INSTANDHALTUNG-MESSSTRECKE-EINTRAGUNG: Maintenance work within the section from the house connection fuse to the metering device, including the metering device, requires the executing party to be the Netzbetreiber or an installation company entered in an installer directory. · NETZBETREIBER-ARBEITSUEBERWACHUNG: The Netzbetreiber is entitled to monitor the execution of the work on the installation. · PLOMBIERUNG-AUSSTATTUNG-DURCH-ANSCHLUSSNEHMER: The Anschlussnehmer must arrange the equipment of the installation required for sealing according to the Netzbetreiber's specifications.
