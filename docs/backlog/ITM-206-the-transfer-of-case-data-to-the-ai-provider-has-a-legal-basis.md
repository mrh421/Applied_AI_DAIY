# ITM-206 The transfer of case data to the AI provider has a legal basis

**REGISTER**

## Outcome

A person with the authority to decide reads the provider's data processing terms and decides on the legal basis of the transfer to a third country (standard contractual clauses, or another basis), or chooses an EU-hosted provider, for which ARC-010 lists Mistral as an alternative. The privacy notice names the result.

## Acceptance

- ARC-010 names the legal basis or the changed provider.
- The privacy notice names the transfer in both variants.
