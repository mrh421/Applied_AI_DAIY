# ITM-266 The screens take the look of the Lovable design

**REGISTER**

## Outcome

The start page, the follow-up questions, the plan with its risk badge, "Who does what", "Check locally", the guide (diagnosis, "Before you start", tools and materials), "Step by step" and "Tools you don't have yet" look as in the six Lovable key screens: Fraunces headings and Manrope text, the green gradient background, the brand header with its tagline, rounded cards with soft shadow, numbered form sections, example chips, risk and label badges. The behaviour of the screens, their texts that carry a safety meaning and their accessible names do not change.

## Acceptance

- Side by side with the six key screens, the screens show the same layout, colours, type and badges.
- The unit, component and system tests pass, including axe, 320 px, 200 percent and keyboard operation.

## Priority

Should: the look of the key screens shown in the presentation (docs/sprint-split.md); planned for sprint 11.
