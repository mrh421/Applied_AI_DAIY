# Fig. 8.2 mapping for slides 23–24

Behind the numbers on the two *143 requirements from 17 sources* slides (before / after). The families are those of Fig. 8.2 (Sommerville, lecture chapter 8). **The assignment is the team's own judgement**, one family per requirement; safety is counted under dependability, as in Sommerville. Everything that says what the app does rather than how well is counted as *functional and domain rules* and has no place in the figure.

*Before* = the 49 requirements of `docs/Requirements_EN.md` v0.4. *After* = the 143 requirements of `SPEC.md`.

| Family | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Usability | 6 | 29 |
| Security & data protection | 2 | 11 |
| Dependability (reliability and safety) | 13 | 33 |
| Efficiency · Performance | 1 | 1 |
| Efficiency · Space | 0 | 0 |
| Beyond Fig. 8.2: functional and domain rules | 27 | 69 |
| **Total** | **49** | **143** |

Efficiency has one requirement (response time, NFR-04 / RESPONSE-TIME) before and after, and none for space: no source asked for it.

Source counts on the *after* slide: team documents 108, prototype 16, laws and standards 35. They add up to 159 because 16 requirements cite two sources (10 team documents + law, 6 team documents + prototype); 92 cite only team documents, 10 only the prototype, 25 only a law or standard.
